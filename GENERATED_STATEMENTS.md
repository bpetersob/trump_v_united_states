### George Washington: A Dangerous Step Toward Monarchy

"It is with a grave and heavy heart that I absorb this Court's decree, which, while professing deference to the law, has nonetheless placed the President in a special class above his fellow citizens [[9]](https://digitalcommons.liberty.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1197&context=ljh)[[10]](https://truthout.org/articles/no-one-should-be-above-the-law/) . I did not fight George III to become George I [[11]](https://www.quora.com/Did-George-Washington-see-himself-as-President-or-King-and-which-did-he-prefer-for-the-future-of-America) . My every action, from insisting on the simple title 'Mr. President' to voluntarily relinquishing power, was to establish that the executive is a servant of the people, not their master [[12]](https://www.mountvernon.org/george-washington/the-first-president/the-farewell-address)[[13]](https://www.mountvernon.org/library/digitalhistory/digital-encyclopedia/article/presidential-precedents)[[14]](https://www.battlefields.org/learn/articles/first-american-president-setting-precedent) . This ruling, by granting 'absolute immunity' for what it deems 'core' powers [[3]](https://www.oyez.org/cases/2023/23-939)[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_v._United_States)[[7]](https://pennlawreview.com/2026/03/06/power-and-immunity-in-youngstown-and-trump-v-united-states/) , creates a shield behind which the very 'cunning, ambitious, and unprincipled men' I warned against may 'usurp for themselves the reins of government' [[12]](https://www.mountvernon.org/george-washington/the-first-president/the-farewell-address)[[15]](https://www.mountvernon.org/library/digitalhistory/past-projects/quotes/article/however-political-parties-may-now-and-then-answer-popular-ends-they-are-likely-in-the-course-of-time-and-things-to-become-potent-engines-by-which-cunning-ambitious-and-unprincipled-men-will-be-enabled-to-subvert-the-power-of-the-people-and-to-usurp-for-th) . To declare any official act entirely beyond the reach of criminal law is to invite the 'spirit of encroachment' that consolidates power and creates 'a real despotism' [[16]](https://oll.libertyfund.org/quotes/george-washington-warns-the-nation-in-his-farewell-address-that-love-of-power-will-tend-to-create-a-real-despotism-in-america-unless-proper-checks-and-balances-are-maintained-to-limit-government-power-1796) . We did not cast off a king only to see the judiciary fashion a new one, cloaked in the robes of 'official acts.' This is a betrayal of the principle that the Constitution is 'sacredly obligatory upon all' [[17]](https://www.nationalreview.com/corner/george-washington-on-respecting-the-constitution-as-written/)[[18]](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/washing.asp) ."


### Thomas Jefferson: The Judiciary Forges New Chains

"I can scarcely express the horror I feel, though I cannot claim to feel surprise . I have long warned that the judiciary is a 'despotic branch,' working like gravity to advance its 'noiseless step like a thief, over the field of jurisdiction' [[32]](https://www.facebook.com/groups/339317480411211/posts/1838450463831231/) . Now, this 'subtle corps of sappers and miners' has used its un-elected power not only to place itself above the other branches, but to shield the executive from the law as well [[33]](https://drive.google.com/a/google.com/open?id=1CGisLAAYwnlQ134sUukRLhNGCAIVxvGO) . They have made the Constitution 'a mere thing of wax' to be twisted into any shape they please [[34]](https://encyclopediavirginia.org/primary-documents/letter-from-thomas-jefferson-to-spencer-roane-september-6-1819/) . The Court claims it must prevent a 'chilled' executive [[35]](https://journals.law.harvard.edu/jlpp/the-presidential-immunity-decision-robert-delahunty-john-yoo/)[[5]](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/23-939_e2pg.pdf) , but this is a preposterous assault on republican virtue. A public servant *should* be chilled by the prospect of breaking the law! When I pursued the prosecution of my own former Vice President, Aaron Burr, for treason, it was from the conviction that no man, regardless of station, is above the law [[36]](https://irlaw.umkc.edu/context/faculty_works/article/1848/viewcontent/The_Treason_Trial_of_Aaron_Burr.pdf)[[37]](https://constitutioncenter.org/blog/the-great-trial-that-tested-the-constitutions-treason-clause)[[38]](https://www.britannica.com/today-in-history/May-22-Aaron-Burrs-Treason-Trial-Begins) . Even when I myself acted beyond the enumerated powers to purchase the Louisiana territory, I did so with the full understanding that an executive must 'throw himself on the justice of his country' [[39]](https://www.americanyawp.com/text/07-the-early-republic/)[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Purchase)[[41]](https://drive.google.com/a/google.com/open?id=1QoQPtsEWL1-o3Yi430myawIFC3muPEXGKg6ElbMCs8U) . This ruling does the opposite; it removes that risk and in so doing, dissolves accountability. The 'despotism of an oligarchy' I so feared has arrived, and it has come to protect the very 'elective despotism' I warned against [[42]](https://teachingamericanhistory.org/document/letter-to-william-torrance/)[[43]](https://drive.google.com/a/google.com/open?id=1P4ZpJ5pi4t0L9xO162vrx6Qw2tLbj1lSJ7-b1IISsVs)[[44]](https://press-pubs.uchicago.edu/founders/documents/v1ch10s9.html) ."


### John Adams: A Government of Categories, Not of Laws

"This is not a ruling; it is a demolition. It is the destruction of the very foundation upon which we sought to build this new world: 'a government of laws, and not of men' [[64]](https://constitutioncenter.org/education/classroom-resource-library/classroom/6.5-primary-source-john-adams-thoughts-on-government-1776) . In the Massachusetts Constitution, I labored to erect 'a government of laws, and not of men' by meticulously separating the powers of government [[65]](https://www.mass.gov/guides/john-adams-the-massachusetts-constitution)[[66]](https://allthingsliberty.com/2022/05/john-adams-and-the-rule-of-law/) . This principle is the bedrock of liberty, ensuring that law is a neutral, objective force [[67]](https://www.acslaw.org/inbrief/what-is-the-rule-of-law/)[[66]](https://allthingsliberty.com/2022/05/john-adams-and-the-rule-of-law/) . This Court has now replaced that simple, elegant principle with a complex and dangerous framework of tiers and presumptions [[7]](https://pennlawreview.com/2026/03/06/power-and-immunity-in-youngstown-and-trump-v-united-states/) . To declare that a President is absolutely immune for some acts [[3]](https://www.oyez.org/cases/2023/23-939)[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_v._United_States) , presumptively immune for others [[4]](https://ballotpedia.org/Trump_v._United_States) , and accountable for a third category is to make the law's power dependent not on its own majesty, but on the nature of the alleged crime. Even when I, facing a quasi-war, signed the Alien and Sedition Acts, those were laws passed by Congress to meet a perceived national security crisis, not a judicial invention of immunity [[68]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_and_Sedition_Acts)[[69]](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/adams-alien-and-seditions-act/)[[43]](https://drive.google.com/a/google.com/open?id=1P4ZpJ5pi4t0L9xO162vrx6Qw2tLbj1lSJ7-b1IISsVs)[[70]](https://pandcvol1redesign.s3.amazonaws.com/P%26C/John+Adams+and+the+Alien+and+Sedition+Act/Handout+A+John+Adams+and+the+Alien+and+Sedition+Acts.pdf) . They were fiercely contested, and rightly so, but they did not place the President himself beyond the law's reach. This ruling does. We have exchanged the clarity of law for the arbitrary complexity of judicial tiers, and in so doing, we have lost the essence of a free government."


### James Madison: The Shattering of the Constitutional Architecture

"If men were angels, no government would be necessary [[103]](https://billofrightsinstitute.org/primary-sources/federalist-no-51/)[[104]](https://constitutioncenter.org/the-constitution/historic-document-library/detail/james-madison-federalist-no-51-1788) . This ruling, however, proceeds from the naive assumption that a President, shielded by layers of immunity, will remain virtuous. The entire structure of our Constitution is a deliberate defense against the predictable 'encroaching spirit of power' [[105]](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/fed48.asp)[[106]](https://ballotpedia.org/Federalist_No._48_by_James_Madison_(1788)) . We designed a system where 'ambition must be made to counteract ambition,' giving each department the 'constitutional means' to resist the others [[103]](https://billofrightsinstitute.org/primary-sources/federalist-no-51/)[[107]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federalist_No._51) . To grant the executive absolute immunity for 'core' powers [[3]](https://www.oyez.org/cases/2023/23-939)[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_v._United_States)[[7]](https://pennlawreview.com/2026/03/06/power-and-immunity-in-youngstown-and-trump-v-united-states/)  and a high wall of 'presumptive' immunity for other official acts [[4]](https://ballotpedia.org/Trump_v._United_States)[[8]](https://www.law.virginia.edu/node/2202121)  is to strip the judiciary of its constitutional means to check the most dangerous branch. It dismantles this carefully constructed edifice, rendering our 'parchment barriers' useless [[106]](https://ballotpedia.org/Federalist_No._48_by_James_Madison_(1788))[[108]](https://teachingamericanhistory.org/document/federalist-no-48-2/) . The President, now unburdened by the full force of criminal law for his official conduct, is no longer one of three co-equal branches, but a power superior to the law itself in certain domains. This is not a mere interpretation of our design; it is its subversion, creating the very 'tyranny' of concentrated power I warned against [[109]](https://civics.asu.edu/node/621)[[110]](https://ballotpedia.org/Federalist_No._47_by_James_Madison_(1788)) ."


### Alexander Hamilton: An Energetic Executive, Not an Imperial One

"I have always argued for an 'energy in the Executive' as essential to good government and the 'security of liberty' [[120]](https://billofrightsinstitute.org/primary-sources/federalist-no-70/)[[121]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federalist_No._70) . But I have never argued for an executive who stands above the law he is sworn to 'take Care be faithfully executed.' Energy is not a synonym for impunity. A vigorous executive is necessary for the 'steady administration of the laws,' as I demonstrated when confronting the Whiskey Rebellion, not for their violation [[120]](https://billofrightsinstitute.org/primary-sources/federalist-no-70/)[[122]](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/duel-whiskey-rebellion/) . Indeed, I argued that a single executive could be watched 'more narrowly' and held responsible [[123]](https://publius.academy/what-is-federalist-70-about)[[120]](https://billofrightsinstitute.org/primary-sources/federalist-no-70/)[[124]](https://teachingamericanhistory.org/document/the-federalist/) . This ruling contorts that principle into a license for unaccountability. I explicitly contrasted our President—who is liable to impeachment, removal, and 'afterwards...prosecution and punishment in the ordinary course of law'—with the British king, whose person is 'sacred and inviolable' [[125]](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/fed69.asp)[[126]](https://talkingpointsmemo.com/edblog/yes-hamilton-thought-john-roberts-was-full-of-it-too)[[127]](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/federalist-no-69-the-real-character-the-executive) . By granting any form of immunity for official acts, the Court has blurred this vital distinction, creating a President who is not energetic, but imperial; not a guardian of the law, but its master in certain realms. This is a grave distortion."
